---
### 📌 TLDR

> [!note]- Click to expand
> - Every **16s** (first time after **8s**) creates **2 sculptures**. If not interrupted – sculptures attack, dealing very high damage.
>
> - Triggering ✨ {SG|sg} during creation – sculpture turns into a harmless statue. Each statue: deals **500k** damage to boss + lowers RES (:anemo:{Anemo|anemo} **-25%**, all other RES **-20%**, stacks up to **3** times). 
>
> - :cryo:{Cryo|cryo} attacks and slowdown. Summons :anemo:{Anemo|anemo} whirlwinds at the character's position – try to step away from the boss at this moment.   

---

> ✧ Killing the boss with ✨ {Stellar Glimmer|sg} eactions is significantly easier – without them you will suffer!  

## ⁘ Main mechanics
1. The boss starts with **90%** all RES right away. **8 seconds** into the fight, and every subsequent **16s**, Churin begins forming **2 sculptures** (creation time _~4s_). If the process is not interrupted, the resulting sculptures will start attacking (once every _~10s_), dealing very high damage. During the fight, this mechanic repeats _6–7_ times.  
<br>    

2. If you apply the Radiance effect (from ✨ {SG|sg} reactions) to the boss before creation finishes, the sculpture turns into a harmless statue. After _1.5s_ it shatters, dealing **500k** damage to the boss and applying **1 stack of Vulnerability**. Each stack lowers :anemo:{Anemo|anemo} ES by **25%** and all other RES by **20%**, stacking up to **3** times. All stacks last _30s_; applying a new stack refreshes the duration.  

## ⁘ Attacks and patterns
- Churin attacks with :cryo:{Cryo|cryo} and applies a slow – this makes the fight harder for melee characters due to decreased movement and attack speed.  
- Summons static :anemo:{Anemo|anemo} whirlwinds at the character's position. Although the boss moves around the arena, you'll have to keep your distance during the whirlwind summon and control where it appears. Otherwise, due to Churin's small size, he'll end up right inside the whirlwind zone, and you won't be able to approach him. After the first whirlwind, summon timings become fixed.  
- Different attack patterns are used depending on your distance from the boss. At the start of the fight, you should close the distance early, but don't get point-blank (because of the whirlwinds), since the ranged attack pattern can disrupt the sculpture-creation timing.  

---

### 💡 Notes:

> [!note]- Click to expand
> - The damage from destroying sculptures is the same on any difficulty  
> - Churin can be frozen for a short period  
> - Testers noted strange boss behavior (possibly a bug): sometimes he would just stand in place and do nothing  
> - In tests, the number of block summons varied from 6 to 7. After the very first summon, periodically, the next one came after 12s instead of 16. Possible reasons: the same strange behavior, or freeze uptime on the boss (depending on the comp)  
> - Without Stellar comps, due to the boss's mechanics, attempts to brute-force D6 will result in a significant cost increase. And it still won't be accessible to just any team  
> - (7.1) Even though this Stygian features two bosses designed around SG, you should prioritize Churin, since Snowmaiden is much easier to clear by comparison. You can put a weaker second Stellar comp there (if you have one), or comfortably do without one entirely

# ◈ Secret Source Automaton: Overseer Device (Cryo Automaton) ◈


| ![[images/Stygian_Automaton_Cryo_en.png\|500]] |
| ------------------------------------- |
| _Click image to zoom in_ |

> ❤️ **HP:** Fearless (D5) – *7.6M* | Dire (D6) – *14.7M*  
> :cryo:{Cryo|cryo} characters  ⠀    ✅

---

### 📌 TLDR

> [!note]- Click to expand
> - After _~7s_ enters enhanced state: All RES **270%**, a gauge appears (32/135, fills **+1/0.4s**). Each :cryo:{Cryo|cryo} attack reduces it by **2**. Beams rotate around the boss, accelerating as the gauge fills.  
>
> - Drop the gauge to **10** → beams stop, RES: :cryo:{Cryo|cryo} **-30%**, others **30%**. Once per phase, **36** energy drops.
>
> - Active character in Nightsoul's Blessing state fills a 2nd gauge (**+10/s**, max **180**). If capped while the main gauge > 10 → special mode: RES **110%**, vertical beams instead of rotating ones. Gauge < **10** → attacks stop and RES drops.
>
> - ЧерезAfter _~65s_, the mode ends, RES **10%**. _~15s_ later – enhanced state again, **+36** energy. 

---

> ✧ "How much :cryo:{Cryo|cryo} do you want?" – Yes.

## ⁘ Main mechanics
1. _~7_ seconds into the fight, the boss enters an enhanced state. All RES increases to **270%**, and a special gauge appears under the HP bar, filled to **32/135** points, which passively charges (1 unit every **0.4s**). Each :cryo:{Cryo|cryo} attack reduces it by **2** units. In this mode, beams rotate around the boss, accelerating and dealing more damage as the gauge fills. 
<br>

	Your task is to drop the gauge to **10** using frequent :cryo:{Cryo|cryo} attacks. Once reached, the beams stop completely, RES drops to **-30%** :cryo:{Cryo|cryo} and **30%** for all other elements. Additionally, once per phase, **36** energy spawns as Clear Energy Particles.  
	
	<br>
	
2. Besides the main gauge, a second gauge fills by **10** units per second (max **180**, initially **30**) while the active character is in the Nightsoul's Blessing state. It appears as a glowing ring around the Automaton's chassis. If it caps out while the main gauge is above **10**, the boss enters an alternate mode.
<br>

	All RES drops to **110%**, and the boss begins firing vertical beams, which also spawn more frequently as the main gauge fills. The rotating beams are disabled during this time. When the gauge drops to **10** or below, the attacks cease and RES is reduced.  
	
	<br>
	
3. After _~65s_, the special mode ends completely, RES drops to **10%**, and the Automaton starts attacking normally. However, after _~15s_ it returns to its the initial state, and _~5s_ later **36** energy drops once more.

---

### 💡 Notes:

> [!note]- Click to expand
> - You can choose which character catches the dropped energy particles  
> - Towards the end of the intermediate phase, prepare for the next rotation in advance, accounting for the energy drop  
> - Cryo Swirl and the Superconduct reactions also lower the gauge   
> - Due to hitlag, phase durations may extend by a few seconds

# ◈ Guardian Blade of Drifting Snow (Snowmaiden) ◈


| ![[images/Stygian_Snowmaiden_en.png\|500]] |
| ------------------------------- |
| _Click image to zoom in_ |

> ❤️ **HP:** Fearless (D5) – _10.3M_ | Dire (D6) – _21.9M_  
> ✨ {Stellar Glimmer|sg} reactions | :electro:{Electro|electro}, :anemo:{Anemo|anemo}, :pyro:{Pyro|pyro} characters  ⠀    ✅

---

### 📌 TLDR

> [!note]- Click to expand
> - At **80% HP**: teleports to the center, over _~5s_ casts a **42U** :cryo:{Cryo|cryo} shield (effectively Frozen). All RES rises to **210%** during cast. While the shield is active – AoE :cryo:{Cryo|cryo} damage.
>
> - If not destroyed within **20s** → **+10%** HP. Triggering ✨ {SG|sg} reaction converts the shield to a regular one (white) = **15%** of max HP. 
>
> - Breaking :cryo:{Cryo|cryo} shield → removes **10%** HP. The regular one → the boss loses **10%** HP + **60%** of the damage dealt to the shield (max **20%** HP).
>
> - After shield break → stun **10s**, RES drops to **-40%**. Final shield phase triggers _~30s_ later.

---

> ✧ ✨ {Stellar Glimmer|sg} mechanics aren't that crucial on this boss – you can easily get by with standard teams.  

## ⁘ Main mechanics
1. Once HP drops to **80%**, the boss teleports to the center of the arena and, over _~5_ seconds, casts a **42U** :cryo:{Cryo|cryo} shield (which is effectively Frozen). During this window, all RES rises to **210%**. While the shield is active – AoE :cryo:{Cryo|cryo} damage.
<br>

2. Triggering ✨ {SG|sg} reactions converts the elemental shield into a regular one equal to **15%** of the boss's max HP. If any shield isn't destroyed within **20s**, the boss heals for **10%** HP. 
<br>

	- Breaking the :cryo:{Cryo|cryo} shield removes **10%** of Snowmaiden's HP
	- The regular shield – the boss loses **10%** HP plus an extra **60%** of the damage dealt to the shield (capped at **20%** max HP)
	
	<br>

3. After the shield is broken, Snowmaiden is stunned for **10s** and all RES drops to **-40%**. After _~30s_, the shield phase repeats one last time.

---

### 💡 Notes:

> [!note]- Click to expand
> - Since the shield is effectively Frozen, it's easier to break than a Cryo shield  
> - On a team without SG, there's no point "squeezing" damage while the elemental shield is up – only elemental application and gauge depletion strength matter. For example, Chevreuse (C4, Q EEE) chips away _~40%_ of the shield, Bennett handles it in ~15s  
> - After an SG reaction, the boss technically gains a barrier, meaning you can trigger Favonius and Sacrificial weapon passives. However, Melt and Vape reactions still cannot be triggered (not entirely sure about this point)   
> - If you convert the shield with SG after already depleting a portion of the elemental shield, the progress carries over    
> - (7.1) As for team comps, prioritize Churin first – SG characters are far more important there. Snowmaiden's mechanics punish non-SG teams significantly less
